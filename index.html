Home / Services / Editorial

Editorial Services

Professional editing that sharpens your manuscript while preserving your unique voice and creative vision.

← Back to All Services

Our editorial team works closely with you through every round of revision to ensure your manuscript meets professional publishing standards. Whether you need a full developmental overhaul or a final proofread before launch, our editors bring the expertise and attention your work deserves.

Developmental Editing

Big picture editing that addresses structure, pacing, character development, narrative arc, plot consistency, and overall story architecture. We identify what works, what needs strengthening, and how to bring your vision to its fullest potential.

Line Editing

Sentence level editing that refines your prose for style, clarity, rhythm, and flow. We tighten your writing without flattening your voice, ensuring every paragraph reads with precision and impact.

Copyediting

Detailed editing for grammar, spelling, punctuation, syntax, consistency, and factual accuracy. We apply style guide standards and ensure your manuscript is technically clean and publication ready.

Proofreading

The final quality check before your book goes to print or digital distribution. We catch remaining typos, formatting errors, and inconsistencies that other editing rounds may have missed.

Manuscript Assessment

A comprehensive written report evaluating your manuscript's strengths, weaknesses, market positioning, and areas for improvement. Ideal for authors seeking professional feedback before committing to a full edit.

Query Letter and Synopsis Development

Professionally crafted query letters and synopses that present your manuscript in its strongest light. We help you distill your story into compelling summaries that capture attention.

Our Process

A straightforward, email based workflow designed to respect your time and deliver professional results.

How It Works

Four Steps From First Email to Final Delivery

Every project and conversation at Legavora is handled entirely by email. No phone calls, no video meetings, no complicated onboarding. Just clear, professional communication.

1

Send Us an Email

Reach out to hello@legavora.com with details about your project. Tell us about your manuscript, your goals, your timeline, and any specific services you need. We respond within 24 to 48 hours.

2

Receive a Custom Quote

After reviewing your project details, we send a detailed proposal tailored to your specific needs. Every quote is custom built. There are no fixed packages or hidden fees.

3

We Get to Work

Once you approve the quote, our team begins work immediately. You receive regular email updates throughout the process so you always know where your project stands.

4

Deliver and Launch

You receive polished, professional deliverables ready for publication and promotion. We remain available by email for any follow up questions or additional support.

Our Philosophy

Why Email Only

Authors work across time zones, schedules, and creative rhythms. Email respects that flexibility. You can send us your questions, files, and feedback whenever it suits you, and we respond with the same care and attention.

Email also creates a clear written record of every decision, revision, and deliverable. Nothing gets lost in a phone call or a forgotten meeting. Every detail of your project is documented and accessible.

This approach allows our team to focus on the work itself rather than scheduling logistics, which means faster turnaround times and higher quality results for every project we take on.

About Legavora

Who We Are

Legavora is an author services and publishing support company built for writers who want their work to meet professional industry standards. We provide editorial, design, marketing, publishing, video production, and career strategy services to authors at every stage of their journey.

Our team includes specialists in editing, book design, illustration, marketing, video production, and publishing logistics. Every project is handled by email, and every quote is custom built around the specific needs of your manuscript.

Our Approach

We believe that professional publishing support should be accessible, transparent, and straightforward. That is why every conversation and project at Legavora is handled by email. No phone calls, no video meetings, no complicated onboarding processes. Just clear, professional communication that respects your time and creative process.

We do not offer fixed packages or standard pricing because no two manuscripts are the same. Every quote we provide is tailored to your specific project, your goals, and your budget. We want you to pay for exactly what you need and nothing you do not.

We focus on the six core service areas where our team delivers the strongest results: editorial, design and production, publishing, marketing and brand, video and social promotion, and career strategy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about working with Legavora.

What services does Legavora offer?▼
Legavora offers six core services: editorial (developmental editing, line editing, copyediting, proofreading, manuscript assessment, query letters), design and production (cover design, children's book illustration, interior layout, formatting, brand identity), publishing guidance (platform setup, distribution, ISBN, launch coordination), marketing and brand (Amazon optimization, keyword strategy, social media planning, email marketing, author websites), video and social promotion (book trailers, BookTok, Instagram Reels, TikTok), and career strategy (roadmap development, genre positioning, platform growth).
What services does Legavora NOT offer?▼
Legavora does not provide ghostwriting, media and press outreach, or library and bookstore distribution. We focus on our six core service areas to maintain the highest quality. If you need these services, we are happy to recommend trusted partners.
How do I get a quote for my project?▼
Email us at hello@legavora.com with details about your project, including your manuscript type, the services you need, your timeline, and any other relevant information. We respond with a custom quote within 24 to 48 hours. You can also use the form on our Contact page.
Do you have fixed pricing or packages?▼
No. Every project is unique, so every quote is custom built around your specific manuscript, goals, and budget. We do not believe in one size fits all packages. When you email us, we provide a detailed proposal tailored to exactly what you need.
Why does Legavora handle everything by email?▼
Email respects your time and creative schedule. Authors work across different time zones and routines, and email allows you to communicate whenever it suits you. It also creates a clear written record of every decision and deliverable, so nothing gets lost.
How long does a typical project take?▼
Timelines vary depending on scope and services needed. A single editing round may take a few weeks, while a full publishing package could take several months. Your custom quote will include a detailed timeline.
Do you work with first time authors?▼
Absolutely. Legavora works with authors at every stage of their career, from first time writers to established authors. We tailor every project to your experience level and goals.
Can I hire Legavora for just one service?▼
Yes. You can hire us for any single service or any combination. Whether you need just a proofread, just a cover, or a full package, we build your quote around exactly what you need.
Is Legavora a literary agent or publisher?▼
No. Legavora is an author services and publishing support company. We provide professional services to help you prepare, publish, and promote your book. We do not publish books under our own imprint.
How do I verify an email is from Legavora?▼
Every Legavora outreach email comes from a team member listed on our Team page. If you receive a message from someone not listed there, contact hello@legavora.com to verify before responding.

Still have questions? Email us and we will get back to you within 24 to 48 hours.
