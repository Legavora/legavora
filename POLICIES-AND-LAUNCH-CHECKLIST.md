# Legavora — legal and compliance launch checklist (November 2026)

Company: LEGAVORA GLOBAL SERVICES LTD, CAC RC 10009828, Nigeria. Registration is not government endorsement.

## Pages included
Privacy, Terms, Refunds & Cancellations, Payments & Billing, Publishing & Distribution, Copyright, Cookies, Accessibility. All are **pre-launch drafts**, not legal advice or ready-to-publish policies.

## Must finish before launch
- Qualified legal review for Nigeria and client markets; confirm Nigerian data protection obligations and relevant consumer protection rules.
- Confirm company legal address, business contact email, support process, effective dates, and applicable tax information.
- Verify refund rules, actual service milestones, processing times and third-party costs against signed contracts.
- Document actual data processors, transfer arrangements, retention periods, user rights process, and cookie inventory.
- Implement consent controls if using nonessential cookies, plus an accessible preference-management mechanism.
- Implement real account security, privacy and file retention controls.
- Obtain approved payment gateway credentials and verify USD settlement before enabling payment collection.
- Obtain official Legavora Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube and TikTok URLs; populate lib/socials.ts with verified links only.
- Use only licensed photographs, videos, trailers and client portfolio materials.
- Do not describe Legavora as government-endorsed or government-approved based solely on CAC incorporation.
- Test all forms, accessibility, data handling and policies against actual production behavior.
